About me

Hello everyone, welcome to my channel!

Hi! My name is Axel and I live in Australia. I am a kid who loves playing video games, especially Minecraft.

I like building bases, finding diamonds, making secret redstone doors, and sometimes accidentally blowing up my own house with TNT. It was just a test, I promise.

I also enjoy mixing coding and Minecraft ideas together because maybe one day I want to make my own game.

Things I like

  • Learning about Minecraft potions and what they do
  • Reading Diary of a Minecraft Zombie
  • Watching Minecraft YouTube videos
  • Doing a bit of coding
  • Maths and Wordle every day because it feels like solving puzzles
  • Making cool ideas for games

Funny facts

  • I once mined for 2 hours and found no diamonds, but I found 64 gravel.
  • My Minecraft dog fell in lava and I am still emotionally recovering.
  • I tried making a secret base and forgot where I hid it.
  • I say "just one more block" and then suddenly it is bedtime.
  • Never dig straight down. Never trust a creeper. Always bring extra torches.

What I want to do

  • Share my videos
  • Show my Minecraft builds
  • Learn more coding
  • Maybe become a YouTuber someday
  • Have fun and make cool things

If you like Minecraft too, then you are automatically cool. Thanks for visiting my website.
