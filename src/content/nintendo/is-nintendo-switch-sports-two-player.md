**Yes. Nintendo Switch Sports supports two players on the same console. Some sports also allow three or four local players, but the limit depends on the sport.** Nintendo's [player and controller guide](https://www.nintendo.com/sg/switch/as8s/guide/index.html) is the useful reference when planning a family match.

## Which sports can the family play together?

| Sport | Maximum players on one console |
| --- | --- |
| Badminton, Chambara, Soccer | 2 |
| Volleyball, Bowling, Tennis, Golf, Basketball | 4 |

These are local limits, not the number of competitors you might see in an online match. Basketball and Golf require the relevant software updates, so update the game before an evening when everyone expects those sports to be ready. Nintendo's [current game listing](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/nintendo-switch-sports-114528/) includes all eight sports.

## Check controllers before choosing a sport

Use compatible Joy-Con controllers; Nintendo Switch Pro Controllers do not work with this game. The controller arrangement varies by sport, so two players should not assume one shared pair covers every option. Check the prompts and Nintendo's [controller FAQ](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/58596/~/nintendo-switch-sports-faq) before starting.

On Nintendo Switch 2, Joy-Con 2 controllers do not fit the Leg Strap. The FAQ explains which original Joy-Con and strap setup is needed for that option.

## A practical first family match

Select **Play Locally** for a same-room session. Start with one sport and take a practice round before counting wins. Badminton is a straightforward choice for two people who want an individual opponent; team sports need an extra conversation about who does what.

Leave room between players and furniture, and wear the controller wrist straps. For a child learning motion controls, practise a small controlled movement before a full match. If competition gets frustrating, swap partners or make the next round about learning one movement instead of keeping a score.
