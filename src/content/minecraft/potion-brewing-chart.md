## Three things to remember

- **Awkward first:** water bottle + nether wart makes the base for most recipes. Weakness starts directly from a water bottle instead.
- **Choose time or strength:** redstone extends a supported potion; glowstone strengthens a supported potion. You cannot combine both upgrades.
- **Change the form last:** add gunpowder for a splash potion, then dragon’s breath for a lingering potion.

The table uses **drinkable bottle timers**, displayed as minutes:seconds. Java and Bedrock share these ingredient paths; **J** and **B** mark different timers for Regeneration and Poison. Splash and lingering potions have different delivered durations, so check their tooltips and remember that splash impact distance matters. Bedrock also supports potion-filled cauldrons; Java does not.

Turtle Master trades movement speed for protection: normal gives Resistance III and Slowness IV; its enhanced version gives Resistance IV and Slowness VI. The four Tricky Trials recipes need Minecraft 1.21 or later. This reference covers vanilla Survival brewing; Mundane and Thick potions have no useful effect-potion branch, and Luck and Decay cannot be brewed in Survival.
