Chaos Cubed arrived in **Minecraft Java 26.2** and **Bedrock 26.30** on 16 June 2026. Its big idea is simple: find a Sulfur Cube, give it different blocks, then see how its movement changes. The drop also adds sulfur caves, Cinnabar and sulfur building sets, Potent Sulfur, springs, geysers and the *Bounce* music disc.

This hub brings our Chaos Cubed guides into one beginner-friendly trail. Start with the question you are trying to solve, or follow the suggested order below.

## A family-friendly way to test Chaos Cubed

Make a fenced test lane away from houses, pets and valuable storage. Put one cube in the lane, change only one block at a time, and write down what happens. Use a non-explosive block first; save TNT for a copied world or an empty area with an adult supervising younger players.
