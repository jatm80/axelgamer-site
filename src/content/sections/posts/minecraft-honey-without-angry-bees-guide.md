A bee nest dripping with honey looks like free treasure. Rush in with a bottle, though, and every bee nearby may zoom straight at you. One smoky campfire lets you harvest the hive without starting a tiny buzzing battle.

This method works in both Java Edition and Bedrock Edition. A bee nest is the natural block found on some trees. A beehive is the version players can craft. The harvesting steps work for both.

## What you need

Bring one campfire and either a glass bottle or a pair of shears. Use the bottle if you want honey. Use shears if you want honeycomb, which you can turn into beehives, candles and other useful items.

Wait until the nest or hive is full. You can spot this without opening any menus. A full one has honey showing in its holes and little golden drops underneath. Bees fill it by visiting flowers, carrying nectar home and working inside.

Clear away nearby leaves, wool and wooden decorations before lighting the campfire. Fire can spread when that game rule is switched on.

## Harvest it step by step

1. Dig a one block hole directly under the nest or hive.

2. Place the campfire in the hole. Check that its smoke rises into the bee home. Do not block the smoke with a solid block.

3. Give the bees room to enter and leave through the front. Try not to stand in their flight path.

4. Use an empty glass bottle on the full nest to collect one honey bottle. If you want honeycomb instead, use shears and collect the three pieces that pop out.

5. Look at the nest again. The visible honey is gone because harvesting resets its honey level. Leave the flowers and bees alone while they refill it.

6. Put out the campfire with a water bottle or a shovel when you finish. You can relight it later with flint and steel when the hive is full again.

## A few bee friendly tips

Never break an occupied nest just to reach the honey. Breaking one without the right precautions releases angry bees, and moving a nest safely requires a tool with the Silk Touch enchantment.

Honey bottles are handy food and can remove the Poison effect when you drink them. Honeycomb is better when you want to grow your bee area with crafted hives. Whichever one you choose, wait for those golden drips first. An early click gives you nothing, while a smoky, patient harvest keeps the whole garden peaceful.
