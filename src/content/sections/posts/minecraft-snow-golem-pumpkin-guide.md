A pumpkin suddenly popping off a snowman to reveal a goofy little face is one of the funniest surprises in Minecraft. Better still, you can build the snowman yourself with only three blocks.

## What you need

Collect two snow blocks, one pumpkin and a pair of shears. Shears are the tool that looks like scissors.

You can craft each snow block from four snowballs. To carve the pumpkin, hold the shears and use them on a normal pumpkin. Pick up the carved pumpkin when you are ready to build.

Choose a cool, dry spot. Snow golems take damage from rain, water and hot places such as deserts and the Nether. A roof keeps the rain away, while a fence helps stop your new mob from wandering off.

## Build your snow golem

1. Place one snow block on the ground.
2. Put the second snow block directly on top.
3. Place the carved pumpkin on top last.

The blocks instantly turn into a snow golem. The pumpkin must be placed last, or nothing happens. A jack o lantern also works if you want to use one instead.

These steps work in both Java Edition and Bedrock Edition. The buttons and menus may look different on each device, but the three block build is the same.

## Meet your snowy helper

A snow golem throws snowballs at nearby monsters. This can distract them, but do not treat the golem like a powerful guard. Its snowballs usually knock monsters without hurting them. Blazes are the useful exception because snowballs damage them.

Give your golem a sheltered yard rather than sending it into a huge battle. As it walks, it can leave thin snow layers on suitable ground. Bedrock Edition has extra limits in warm biomes, so a snow trail may not appear there.

Now for the best bit. Use shears on the snow golem's pumpkin head. The carved pumpkin drops as an item and the golem's funny real face appears underneath. This works in Java and Bedrock.

Try building a tiny snowy garden with a roof, spruce fences and two golems. You will get a moving snow trail, a pair of ridiculous faces and a base visitor that is much stranger than an ordinary snowman.
