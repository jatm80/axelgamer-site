A sign beside a dark mine can be very easy to miss. Add one splodge of glowing ink, though, and the words suddenly pop out with a bright outline. It is a tiny Minecraft trick that can make a busy base much easier to explore.

This works on normal signs and hanging signs in both Java Edition and Bedrock Edition. The glowing words do not light nearby blocks or stop mobs appearing. They simply stay easier to read when the area is dark.

## What you need

* One placed sign or hanging sign
* One glow ink sac
* One dye in a colour you like
* One normal ink sac if you want to undo the glow
* One honeycomb if you want to lock the finished message

Glow squids drop glow ink sacs. Search underwater, watch your air bubbles and return to the surface before they run out. If you are still learning survival mode, explore with an older player rather than diving into a deep cave alone.

## Make the words glow

1. Place your sign and type a short message. Four clear lines are much easier to read than a giant squashed sentence.

2. Hold your chosen dye and use it on the sign. This changes all the plain text on that face to the dye colour.

3. Hold a glow ink sac and use it on the same face. The letters gain an outline and become much clearer in low light.

4. Walk a few blocks away and check your work during the night or inside a dim room. Try another dye if the first colour blends into the wood.

5. If you dislike the glow, use a normal ink sac on that face to remove it. Breaking the sign also removes its decorated text, so edit it instead when possible.

## Use both sides

Signs can hold different writing on the front and back. Walk around the sign and use the empty side to open its editor. You could write "Mine this way" on one face and "Base this way" on the other.

Dye and glow ink are applied to one face at a time. Decorate both sides if players will approach from both directions.

## Lock an important message

When everything looks right, use a honeycomb on the sign to wax it. Waxing stops players from editing the text. You cannot scrape the wax off, so double check spelling first. If you make a mistake later, break the sign, place it again and rewrite it.

Try glowing signs above storage chests, beside Nether portals or along a tunnel with several turns. They are especially handy in a shared world where one neat label can prevent everyone opening the same six chests to find the cobblestone.
