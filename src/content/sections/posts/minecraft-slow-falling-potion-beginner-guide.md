Stepping off a giant tower usually ends with a thump and a frantic search for your dropped gear. A Potion of Slow Falling changes that. Drink one and you drift down gently, which makes tall builds and mountain trips much less risky.

This recipe works in both Java Edition and Bedrock Edition. The ingredients and potion times are the same in each edition.

## What you need

Gather a brewing stand, blaze powder, one to three water bottles, one nether wart and one phantom membrane. Blaze powder fuels the brewing stand. A phantom membrane is an item dropped by phantoms, the flying mobs that appear at night after you have skipped sleep for several Minecraft days.

You can brew up to three potions in one batch without using extra nether wart or membrane, so filling all three bottle slots is a smart move.

## Brew it step by step

1. Open the brewing stand and place blaze powder in the fuel slot on the left.

2. Put your water bottles in the three bottle slots at the bottom.

3. Place nether wart in the top ingredient slot. Wait for the bubbles to finish. Your bottles will become Awkward Potions, which are the starting point for many useful brews.

4. Put one phantom membrane in the top slot. When brewing finishes, each bottle becomes a Potion of Slow Falling that lasts for 1 minute and 30 seconds.

5. If you want more time, place redstone dust in the top slot. This stretches each potion to 4 minutes. Do not use glowstone dust here because Slow Falling does not have a stronger second level.

## Try it somewhere sensible

Drink the potion before climbing a tall scaffold, exploring a mountain or practising with an elytra. While the effect is active, you fall slowly and take no fall damage when you land.

Test your first bottle from a small ledge where you can see the pink Slow Falling effect icon on your screen. Keep watching the timer. If it runs out while you are still high in the air, normal falling returns.

Phantoms can be annoying to fight, especially for new players. Wear armour, stay near a lit shelter and ask another player to help if needed. Once you have the membrane, brew three bottles at once and save the extras for your next enormous build. That strange flappy mob has just become genuinely useful.
