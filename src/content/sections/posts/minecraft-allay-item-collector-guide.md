An allay looks like a tiny blue fairy that has had too much cordial. It zips about, makes cheerful noises and can help clean up dropped items. The clever part is choosing exactly what you want it to collect.

This works in both Java Edition and Bedrock Edition.

## What you need

* One allay
* One sample item, such as cobblestone, wheat seeds or an egg
* A note block if you want a delivery spot
* A button, lever or redstone clock to play the note block

Allays can be found trapped at pillager outposts and woodland mansions. Freeing one can be risky, so take armour and food. Younger players might want a grown up or an experienced friend beside them.

A note block is a musical block. It makes a sound when a player taps it or redstone powers it.

## Set up your flying helper

1. Hold the item you want collected, then use it on the allay. The allay will hold one copy and start following you.

2. Drop a few matching items on the ground as a test. If you gave it cobblestone, it will search for dropped cobblestone. It cannot mine stone, open chests or steal blocks from your build.

3. Stand still and watch. The allay picks up the matching drops, flies back and tosses them near you.

4. Place a note block where you want deliveries. Play it while the allay is nearby. The allay will use that spot for a while instead of bringing items straight to you.

5. Keep the note block playing now and then if you want the allay to stay linked to it. A button is fine for a quick test. A repeating redstone circuit is handier for a busy farm, but ask before building one on a shared world because constant sounds can annoy other players.

6. To change jobs, use an empty hand on the allay to take back its sample item. Give it a different item and test again.

## Try these easy jobs

Put an allay near a wheat farm to collect loose seeds after harvesting. It can also grab eggs from a chicken pen or blocks left behind while you clear an area. Keep the space enclosed so your helper does not wander into danger.

Remember that an allay only collects items already lying on the ground. It is a speedy tidy up mate, not an automatic miner. Start with a handful of test drops, listen for the happy chimes and see whether your tiny delivery crew gets everything to the right spot.
